# Privacy policy

mailagent is a personal command-line Gmail client. It runs only on the machine of the person who installed it.

- It accesses Gmail solely on behalf of the account that authorises it, using Google's OAuth consent flow.
- Messages, labels and tokens are read and stored locally on that machine. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's Gmail API.
- No data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.
- Revoking access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, or running `mailagent logout`, removes the stored token.
